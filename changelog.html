What's shipped, as it ships.

Every real change to Junie, newest first. Have a request? See the roadmap and vote on what's next.

This week

Labs & bloodwork New

Connect LabCorp, Quest Diagnostics, or Function Health directly. Results now factor into every answer alongside your wearable data — no more "soon."

This week

Family & caregiver sharing New

Give up to 4 people permissioned access to your account, or theirs to you. Set a critical threshold and Junie can notify a second person automatically.

Last week

Clinician & coach report links New

Generate a scoped, read-only link for a specific date range and hand it to your doctor or trainer — no chat transcript, no full account access.

Last week

Nutrition logging New

MyFitnessPal and Cronometer are now connectable sources, so food shows up in the same answers as sleep, training, and recovery.

2 weeks ago

Daily digest, zero setup New

Turn on a morning recap and get a plain-language summary in your inbox. No agent to configure, no separate tool to learn — it just works.

2 weeks ago

International device support Improved

Fitbit and Coros are now fully live. Amazfit, Xiaomi Mi Band, and Huawei Health are in beta for accounts outside the US.

3 weeks ago

Full historical backfill on connect Improved

New sources now pull your complete available history on day one, not just data going forward. Reconnect an existing source to backfill it too.

3 weeks ago

Per-source failure alerts Fixed

When a source stops syncing, Junie now names exactly which one instead of a generic "something's wrong" warning.

Last month

Personal API New

Pull your data as JSON for your own dashboard, spreadsheet sync, or model. Included with Pro — see the developer docs for authentication and rate limits.

Last month

Junie launches New

Connect your rings, watches, CGMs, and gym logs into one private feed any AI can read from — Claude, ChatGPT, or anything that speaks MCP.
