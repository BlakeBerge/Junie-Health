📋 Looking for how we handle health data specifically? That lives in a separate Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy, as required under laws like Washington's My Health My Data Act. This page covers everything else.

1. Who this applies to

This Privacy Policy explains how [Junie Health, Inc.] ("Junie," "we," "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our website, application, and MCP server (the "Service"). For consumer health data specifically, see our separate Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy, which controls where the two overlap.

2. Information we collect

CategoryExamples
Account informationName, email address, password (hashed), plan tier
Connected source dataSleep, HRV, training, glucose, lab results, nutrition logs, and similar data from sources you authorize

3. How we use information

  • To operate the Service — authenticating you, syncing connected sources, and serving your data to the AI assistants you connect;
  • To send you service communications (e.g., your daily digest, if enabled, or account and billing notices);
  • To provide customer support and respond to requests;
  • To maintain security, detect fraud, and enforce our Terms;
  • To improve the Service — for example, understanding which connectors are most requested.

We do not use your data to train Junie's own systems or any third-party AI model, and we do not sell your data.

4. How information is shared

We share information only in these circumstances:

  • With AI assistants you connect — when you query Claude, ChatGPT, or another assistant through your Junie link, your data is sent to that assistant to answer your question, subject to that provider's own terms and privacy policy;
  • With service providers who help us run the Service (hosting, payment processing, email delivery), under contracts limiting their use of your data to providing that service;
  • With a clinician or coach, only when you generate a scoped report link and choose to share it;
  • For legal reasons, if required by law, subpoena, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Junie or others;
  • In a business transfer (merger, acquisition, or asset sale), with notice to you and the same privacy commitments carried forward.

We do not share or sell your data to advertisers or data brokers.

5. Data retention and deletion

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You can export your full history as CSV at any time, and delete your account, connectors, and history — every byte — from your account settings. Deletion is immediate and we don't retain a copy afterward, except where we're legally required to retain limited records (e.g., billing records for tax purposes).

6. Security

We use encryption in transit and at rest, scoped read-only access to connected sources, and maintain an audit log of queries made against your data. No system is perfectly secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security — see Section 9 for what happens if something goes wrong.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export your data, and to opt out of certain processing. You can exercise most of these directly from your account settings, or by contacting us at [privacy@yourdomain.com]. Residents of California, Virginia, Colorado, and other states with comprehensive privacy laws have additional statutory rights; residents of Washington should also see our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy.

8. Children's privacy

The Service is not directed at children under 13 (or the relevant age of digital consent in your country), and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

9. Breach notification

If there's a breach of unsecured, individually identifiable health information, we'll notify affected users, and where required, the FTC and media, consistent with the FTC's Health Breach Notification Rule and applicable state law.

10. International users

If you access the Service from outside [the United States], your information will be transferred to and processed in [the United States]. Where applicable law (such as the GDPR) requires additional safeguards for that transfer, we put them in place.

11. Changes to this policy

We'll post updates here with a new "Last updated" date, and for material changes, notify you by email or in-product notice.

Questions or requests: [privacy@yourdomain.com]
