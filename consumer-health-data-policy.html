1. Scope of this policy

This Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy describes how [Junie Health, Inc.] collects, uses, and shares "consumer health data" as defined under applicable state law, including Washington's My Health My Data Act (RCW 19.373) and similar laws in other states. It applies regardless of your location, but certain rights described below may only be legally guaranteed to residents of specific states.

2. Categories of consumer health data we collect

CategoryDescription
Biometric dataHeart rate, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, sleep stages and duration, blood oxygen, body temperature, and similar measurements from connected devices
Bodily function and condition dataGlucose readings, laboratory and bloodwork results, weight, and similar measurements
Fitness and activity dataWorkouts, training load, exercise logs
Nutrition dataLogged food and macronutrient intake, where you connect a nutrition source
InferencesAny inference drawn from the above to identify your health status or condition (for example, an AI assistant's summary of a trend in your data)

3. Sources of this data

  • Directly from you, when you enter or upload information;
  • From third-party sources you authorize us to connect to (rings, watches, CGMs, labs, gym and nutrition apps);
  • Generated by AI assistants you connect, when they process your data to answer a question.

4. Purpose of collection

We collect and use consumer health data solely to provide the Service you request: consolidating your connected sources, serving that data to the AI assistants you choose, generating the digest or alerts you enable, and maintaining your account.

5. Categories of consumer health data shared, and with whom

RecipientWhat's sharedWhy

We do not sell consumer health data, and we do not share it with advertisers, data brokers, or any third party for advertising purposes.

6. Your rights

  • Right to know and access — confirm whether we're collecting, sharing, or selling your consumer health data, and access it, including a list of third parties it's been shared with.
  • Right to delete — request deletion of your consumer health data, including instructing our service providers to do the same.
  • Right to withdraw consent — revoke a previously granted authorization at any time, without penalty.
  • Right to appeal — if we deny a request, you can appeal and will receive a written response.

To exercise any of these rights, contact [privacy@yourdomain.com]. We will respond without undue delay, and in any case within 45 days (with one possible 45-day extension where reasonably necessary). Verified requests are free up to twice per year.

7. Consent

We obtain your affirmative consent before collecting consumer health data beyond what's necessary to provide a product or service you've requested, and separate affirmative consent before sharing it. If we intend to collect, use, or share new categories of consumer health data, or use existing data for a new purpose, we'll disclose that change and obtain your consent first.

8. Data security

We maintain administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect consumer health data, including encryption in transit and at rest and scoped, read-only access to connected sources.

9. Geofencing

We do not use geofencing around healthcare facilities to collect consumer health data, identify or track consumers seeking health care services, or send notifications related to a consumer's consumer health data or health care services.
